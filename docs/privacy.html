Privacy Policy

Minimal Launcher  ·  Last updated: March 2026

Minimal Launcher is a home-screen replacement app built for focus and simplicity. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and — importantly — what it never does.

Data we access and why

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Approximate location — Used solely on-device to fetch your local weather (temperature, conditions) and to calculate today's sunrise and sunset times. Your coordinates are never stored on any server and are never shared with third parties. The only external call is a standard HTTPS request to the open-source Open-Meteo weather API, which receives your latitude and longitude to return weather data. Open-Meteo does not log or retain individual requests.
  • Calendar — Read-only access to display upcoming events (next 24 hours) on your home screen. Calendar data never leaves your device.
  • Usage statistics (screen time) — Used to calculate and display your daily screen time on the home screen. This data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • App list — Required for a launcher to display and launch your installed apps. The list of installed apps never leaves your device.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not store any data on external servers (beyond the anonymous weather API call).
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.

Data storage

All preferences (pinned apps, settings, blocked apps, name, etc.) are stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

Minimal Launcher is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.
